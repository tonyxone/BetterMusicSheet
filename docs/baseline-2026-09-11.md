That $112/month bought **four sheets in ninety days** — roughly $28 per sheet,
almost entirely for capacity sitting idle.

## What to compare after cutover

- **Recurring cost**, expected to fall from ~$113/month to under $1/month plus
  per-job usage. Check daily granularity, not the monthly total: September is a
  split month and will show both regimes averaged together.
- **p50 duration**, expected to *rise* on the first job after an idle period.
  Measured 163s on the cutover test (102s cold start + 61s recognition) against
  a 125s warm baseline. A warm queue should stay near baseline.
- **Failure rate**, which must not rise. It is 0% today, so any failure after
  cutover is a regression rather than noise.

## Reading cost data after the cutover

Cost Explorer lags roughly a day, so the current date always shows $0.00. That
is missing data, not a saving. Wait for the day to settle before drawing any
conclusion from it.
