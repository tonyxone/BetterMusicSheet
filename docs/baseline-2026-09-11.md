## What this says

Recurring infrastructure is **$16.64/month** — ECS, the ALB, the VPC's public
IPv4 addresses, Route 53 and ECR. The $16.00 registrar charge is the annual
domain renewal, not a monthly cost, and tax follows the total.

That $16.64 bought **four sheets in ninety days**, or about **$4.16 per sheet**,
almost entirely for capacity sitting idle. It is the clearest argument for the
migration: ECS, ELB and the public IPv4 addresses (about $16.13 of it) all
disappear when workers scale to zero, leaving Route 53 at $0.50 plus a few cents
of per-job compute.

## What to compare after cutover

- Recurring monthly cost, expected to fall from $16.64 to roughly $0.50 plus usage.
- p50 duration, expected to **rise** on the first job after an idle period —
  container start and image pull land on top of the 125s. A warm queue should
  stay close to baseline.
- Failure rate, which must not rise. It is 0% today, so any failure after
  cutover is a regression, not noise.
